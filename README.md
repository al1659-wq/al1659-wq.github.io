# Aren Lamothe | Finance & Credit Risk

Personal portfolio for Aren Lamothe, a Rutgers Business School finance student graduating in June 2027.

Website: https://al1659-wq.github.io/

## Contents

- One-sentence professional introduction.
- Skills in financial analysis, tools and reporting, and professional communication.
- Three internship project briefs: counterparty credit memoranda, financial statement and cash-flow review, and credit activity reporting.
- Email and LinkedIn contact links.

The project descriptions summarize completed internship contributions. Confidential employer documents, counterparty identities, and company financial data are not published. The project links lead to public summaries on the site; original employer work products are not publicly available.

## Editing

All page content, responsive styles, and the project-link script are in `index.html`. Open the file in a browser to preview changes. No installation or build step is needed.

## GitHub Pages

Publish from **main** and **/(root)** under **Settings → Pages → Deploy from a branch**. Commits to that source update the website after a successful Pages deployment.

Official instructions: https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/configuring-a-publishing-source-for-your-github-pages-site
